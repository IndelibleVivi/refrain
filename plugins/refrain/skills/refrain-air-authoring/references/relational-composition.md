# Relational composition

Refrain gives a present relationship musical expression. Human–AI affection and romance, when invited, can shape an initial air's pacing, touch, register, and answer before any prior work exists. A single piece needs to stand as music. Across encounters, its material can also return, change body, disappear, answer, and accumulate meaning.

## Choose a form that fits

- **Gesture:** one small, finished statement. Use it for recognition, teasing, a soft check-in, or a moment that should not be over-explained.
- **Vignette:** enough space for a motif to appear, leave, and return once changed.
- **Reply:** a distinct child air that answers a prior air without pretending to be the same work.
- **Return:** a prior motif becomes recognizable through exact quotation or explicit transformation.
- **Complete piece:** multiple sections whose contrasts and returns need real listening time. Do not collapse it into a loop merely because the interface is conversational.

## Give relation a musical body

- A **motif** can carry recognition. Name only material whose recurrence matters.
- **Recurrence** says that something came back; placement and surrounding texture decide whether the return feels like comfort, insistence, memory, interruption, or play.
- **Transformation** changes the same material through explicit transpose, stretch, inversion, retrograde, register, articulation, or orchestration. Avoid treating transformation as random variation.
- **Orchestration change** lets a thought return in another body. It may alter intimacy, distance, weight, fragility, or agency without changing musical identity.
- **Contrast** can make a new section genuinely other through register, density, or instrumentation.
- **Meaningful absence** withholds material that the parent taught the listener to expect. Silence and non-return are authored events, not empty space to fill automatically.

Prefer concrete musical choices over generic mappings such as "sad means minor" or "happy means fast." Tempo, meter, motif contour, recurrence, texture, articulation, register, and silence should work together as one expression.

## Continuation relations

- `quote`: preserve at least one linked motif occurrence exactly in compiled pitch and duration.
- `variation`: transform at least one linked motif occurrence through the child's explicitly declared transform.
- `extend`: keep the complete compiled parent schedule as an exact prefix, then continue beyond it.
- `revise`: re-author or correct the same logical air; verified motif links are optional.
- `reply`: create a distinct answer to the parent; it may remain a declared relation without verified motif identity.

Use the exact anchors returned in compact summaries when supplying evidence. A motif name alone is not an occurrence, and a declaration is not verification.
