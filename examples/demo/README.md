# Two airs

Cove authored these complete AIR works with an Astra host agent. Refrain compiles the explicit notes, form, and original performance bindings; the demo does not request music from a generation service.

- **Velvet Mischief · 夜色偏心** — 108 BPM, 64 bars, 2:22 of music. Piano, marimba, clarinet, cello, bass, percussion, and glass bell. This is the original version.
- **After the Door · 门后** — 96 BPM, 32 bars, 1:20 of music. A returning motif moves through glass, piano, harp, and electronic pulse.

The portable artifacts preserve the music and exact sound choices. After the Door’s public caption omits an obsolete private listening-status sentence; its source, receipt, and binding are unchanged. Included render receipts refer to earlier native/matched WAV and MIDI exports; those files are not bundled here.

These authored works follow the project’s CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 content terms. Instrument samples retain their own asset-level rights; see [sound sources](../../docs/SOUND-SOURCES.md). The build publishes only the exact required sample closure, with source notices and `sound-provenance.json`.
