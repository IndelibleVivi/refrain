## Source audit boundary

- [VCSL](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) is pinned at `c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e`. Its repository-level CC0 text is stored locally, but every used WAV still receives its own path, byte count, digest, audio metadata, and processing record. The current source contains the percussion families used by the acoustic kit as well as the existing piano, harp, and marimba material; it does not contain matching nylon guitar, solo cello, flute, or clarinet sets.
- [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) is pinned at SFZ commit `6dd651d55dde97fd4028699be9d4481f26917891`. Its CC0 text is stored separately under `third_party/VSCO-2-CE/`. The pinned branch contains the source SFZ mappings and samples for orchestral strings, solo contrabass, flute, and clarinet. Source availability is not implementation or listening acceptance.
- [FreePats Spanish Classical Guitar](https://github.com/freepats/spanish-classical-guitar) is acquired from its exact 2019-06-18 SFZ/WAV 7z archive, pinned by byte count and SHA-256 before individual members are extracted and verified. Its CC0 text is stored under `third_party/FreePats-Spanish-Classical-Guitar/`.
- [Karoryfer Bigcat Cello](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer-bigcat.cello) is pinned at `6fd75fbfc1dbb3109bf26220ba1adea46188a18b`. It supplies a true solo cello rather than relabeling an ensemble section; its CC0 text is stored under `third_party/Karoryfer-Bigcat-Cello/`.

## Acceptance boundary

Mechanical acceptance covers schema validation, canonical digests, asset provenance and exact bytes, deterministic selection, full required-asset closure, render receipts, local timings, peaks, exports, and browser execution. Listening acceptance belongs only to Faye and is bound to exact audio digests. Public-release acceptance additionally requires complete identity coverage, asset-level rights closure, clean-install/runtime evidence, and an explicit release decision; neither a green test suite nor a liked bounded probe performs that promotion.
