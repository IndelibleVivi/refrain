## Acceptance boundary

Machine evidence may close semantic, provenance, reproducibility, performance, and interoperability rows. It cannot promote a listening candidate. Faye's recorded G3A decisions apply only to the exact candidate and fallback WAV digests in `listening-decisions.json`; they do not promote partial maps to public-release sound identities.
