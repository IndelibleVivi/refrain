# Atelier artwork

These two composed room illustrations were created for this experiment with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-16. The mobile artwork uses the generated desktop room as its only reference. No external artwork was copied. Source PNGs were encoded to WebP at quality 90; no visual or geometric edits were applied during conversion. Interactive hit regions and engraved playing details are authored in `drawing.ts`; these are artistic interpretations, not mechanically accurate diagrams.

Authored direction and project content follow the existing CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 scope in the root LICENSING.md. This does not claim exclusive rights in machine-generated elements.

## Desktop prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one exquisitely art-directed, coherent fine-pen illustration of a sunlit music atelier, as the actual central scene of a minimalist playable artwork. Landscape 4:3 composition.
This must feel like ONE observed room drawn by ONE master, not separate instrument cutouts pasted together. Consistent perspective, correct relative scale, shared floor, meaningful proximity, overlapping depth, restrained asymmetry. Leonardo da Vinci's delicate silverpoint and pen notebook draftsmanship, contemporary white atelier, fine architectural drawing. Very pale warm grey ink, intricate accurate instrument mechanisms, subtle hand hatching. Pure luminous WHITE ground, white open space, no parchment or yellow paper.
Composition: a grand piano with raised lid occupies the lower-left middle foreground, keyboard facing us and right. A low plain piano bench in front, a tiny sleeping grey cat curled on the bench. A cello on a delicate wooden stand nestles near the piano's right curved side in the lower middle. Behind them to the upper-left stands a marimba, smaller by distance, tone bars and resonators clearly recognizable. A tall graceful harp is just right of centre and behind the cello, full silhouette clear, thin strings catching light. A large airy olive tree in a simple pale earthenware pot rises at the far right, branches reaching INTO the ensemble; a second very small fern pot beside the bench grounds the near-left. All four instruments are complete and clearly distinct, arranged as an intimate chamber ensemble, not in a row or grid.
Space and light: a barely drawn tall window on the back-left wall; late morning sunlight forms one large elegant parallelogram across the white floor, broken by a few window mullion shadows. Soft contact shadows genuinely anchor all objects to this SAME floor. The room disappears into blank white at all four margins. No closed rectangular frame. Let upper-right air and lower margin breathe. Pale muted olive leaves are the ONLY slight color, everything else warm grey/white. No black voids, no brown tint, no glossy rendering.
Beauty comes from sculptural arrangement, cadence of heights and negative space, the piano's curved volume, the harp's delicate verticals, foliage softness, shared sunlight. Editorial fine art, meticulous high-detail drawing, tactile and inhabited, never sterile.
Absolutely NO text, letters, notation labels, diagrams, numbers, buttons, UI, captions, decorative grids or borders. Do not crop any instrument.

## Mobile recomposition prompt

Recompose this SAME atelier illustration for a tall mobile portrait canvas, aspect ratio 3:4. This is an art-directed new portrait composition, NOT a crop or stretched image. Keep the exact visual world: luminous pure white room, fine warm-grey Renaissance pen and silverpoint draftsmanship, delicate instruments, tiny olive color, soft morning sun, detailed contact shadows. Preserve all FOUR instruments clearly and completely: grand piano is the lower-middle/left anchor, keyboard facing lower-left; cello in its stand at lower-right in front of harp; harp standing upper-right behind cello; marimba receding upper-left beside the tall window. The olive tree grows at the back-right and its airy branches embrace the upper-right margins without hiding the harp. Tiny sleeping grey cat on piano bench at near-left, a little fern nearby. Fit EVERY instrument and the whole bench inside the frame, with shared correct perspective and realistic size relationships; a charming intimate room, not a stack of cutouts. A slender vertical composition with floor sunlight drawing the eye down through the ensemble. Modest empty white breathing room at top and bottom, artwork fading softly into pure white at all margins. No writing, letters, numbers, notation, UI, border or caption. Keep detailed fine drawn construction, not a photograph.
